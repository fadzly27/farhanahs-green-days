1Install on your iPhone

You only do this once. After that it works like a normal app on your home screen.

  1. 🧭
    In Safari, tap Open the app (top of this page)It has to be Safari. Chrome on iPhone can't install it properly. Do the next steps on the app page, not on this guide.
  2. Tap the Share buttonThe square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen (tap ⋯ first if you don't see it).
  3. ➕
    Choose Add to Home ScreenIf you can't see it, scroll down a little in that menu.
  4. ✅
    Tap AddThe green leaf icon 🌿 Green Days appears on your home screen.

💡 From now on, always open it from the 🌿 icon, not from Safari. It works even without internet.

2Every day (30 seconds)

Everything is on the Today page. Whatever you tap saves automatically.

  1. 🧋
    Had a sugary drink? Turn on Sugary drinkTeh tarik, Milo ais, sirap, canned drinks, bubble tea…
  2. 🍰
    Had something sweet? Turn on Sweet foodKuih, cake, donuts, cendol, chocolate…
  3. 💧
    Each time you drink water: + Glass or + Bottle1 glass ≈ 250 ml, 1 bottle ≈ 500 ml. Tapped by mistake? Undo
  4. 🥦
    Ate veggies or fruit? Tap the 🥦 boxThe 🍟 box is for fried or processed food.
  5. 🌙
    In the evening: Evening check-inCravings, mood and notes. Optional, never required.
  6. ✅
    A perfect day with nothing to tick? Just tap Save today

↩️ Forgot to fill in yesterday? Tap ‹ next to the date at the top to change the day.

3Day colours

GreenNo sugary drink & no sweet food 💚
YellowJust one of the two. Still a good day 🙂
RedBoth. That's okay, tomorrow is a fresh start 🌱

🔥 The number at the top right is how many green days in a row you have. There's a little surprise at 3, 7, 14, 30, 60 and 100 days 😉

4Counting calories

Already switched on for you, so you'll see a calorie ring at the top of Today. Don't want it? Settings → Diet → turn off Track food & calories.

  1. 🎯
    First time: tap Set a goal on the calorie cardFill in your age, height, weight & activity level, then pick a daily goal. (You can redo it anytime in Settings → Diet → Calculate for me.)
  2. 🍳
    On the Today page, scroll down to Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, SnacksTap + Add food under the right meal.
  3. 🔍
    Search for a food, e.g. “nasi lemak”, “teh o”, “mcd”, “mixue”, “sushi” or “ayam kicap”Over 1,000 Malaysian foods, drinks, kuih, lauk, sushi, fast food and snacks. Tap the round + to add it straight away, or tap the food's name to choose a portion (½, 1, 1½…).
  1. 🍽️
    Build a plate: for home-cooked food or nasi campurNo numbers to type. Pick rice ✊, lauk ✋, veggies 🥦 and oil/sambal 👍 using your hand as a guide, and the app works out the calories.

6Your data & backups

🔒 Everything you log is stored only on your iPhone. No account, and nobody else can see it, not even the person who made this app 😊
  1. 📦
    Once a month: Settings → Export backup (JSON)Choose Save to Files, or send it to yourself.
  2. 📥
    New phone, or lost your data? Import backupPick the backup file and everything comes back.

⚠️ Deleting the app icon, or clearing Safari's history (Clear History and Website Data), also deletes your data. That's why backups matter.

?Questions

The app looks different / is there an update?

The app updates itself. Close it fully (swipe up from the bottom of the screen, then swipe the app away), then open it again. The version number is at the very bottom of the Settings page.

Tapped something by mistake?

Tap it again to switch it off. For water, tap Undo. For a deleted food, tap Undo in the message at the bottom of the screen.

Barcode scanning doesn't work?

Make sure Safari is allowed to use the camera (tap Allow). If it still doesn't work, type the barcode number below the camera. If the product isn't found, enter the calories from the label and the app will remember it next time.

Had a red day?

That's completely okay 🌱 This app isn't here to make you feel guilty. Every day is a fresh start. One green day at a time.
